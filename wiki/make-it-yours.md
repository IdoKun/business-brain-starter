# Make it yours
Source: the starter · Updated: 2026-11-10

Your hub's look lives in one file: `hub/brand.css`. Ask Claude Code to change it. Your pages and search don't change.

**How to ask (good habits):**
1. **Show, don't describe.** Give Claude your real colors: "Use the colors from my website (link)", or paste hex codes (#1f5f55), or a screenshot of a design you like.
2. **One change at a time.** Change it, reload your hub, look. Then the next change.
3. **Ask for options first.** "Show me 3 color options before you change anything."
4. **Undo is free.** Git keeps every version: "Undo the last design change."

**Copy-paste examples (into Claude Code):**
- `Change my hub's colors to my brand: main color #C0392B, background off-white. Only change hub/brand.css. Push to main.`
- `Use the font Inter for my hub, and make the text a bit bigger.`
- `Replace the 🧠 with my logo: [file name in my Drive].`
- `Make dark mode warmer: dark brown instead of dark green.`
- `Undo the last design change.`

The ◐ button (top right) switches light / dark on your computer.
